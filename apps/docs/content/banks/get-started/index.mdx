## Welcome

Thank you for partnering with Vinr! This guide will help you get started with our banking integration platform.

## Overview

As a Vinr bank partner, you'll provide payment processing services to merchants across Bulgaria, Romania, Greece, and Poland through our unified platform.

## Key Benefits

- **Expand Your Reach** - Access to thousands of merchants
- **Modern Infrastructure** - State-of-the-art payment processing
- **Regulatory Compliance** - Built-in compliance tools
- **Real-time Reporting** - Comprehensive transaction reporting

## Getting Started Steps

1. **Complete Onboarding** - Submit required documentation and compliance forms
2. **Get API Credentials** - Receive your sandbox and production credentials
3. **Integrate APIs** - Connect your systems to Vinr
4. **Test Integration** - Validate your integration in sandbox
5. **Go Live** - Launch your production integration

## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:

- Banking license in at least one of our operating countries
- Technical team capable of API integration
- Compliance and risk management systems
- 24/7 operations support capability
