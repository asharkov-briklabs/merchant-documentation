# Bank Partner Documentation

Welcome to Vinr's partner documentation. This guide will help you integrate, configure, and manage Vinr as a white-label payment platform for your bank.

<Callout type="info">
**Partner Access Required:** This documentation is exclusively for Vinr bank partners. If you're a merchant, visit the [Merchant Documentation](/merchant).
</Callout>

## What You Can Do

As a Vinr bank partner, you can:

- 🎨 **White-label the platform** with your bank's branding
- 🏦 **Onboard your merchants** to the payment platform
- 📊 **Access aggregated reporting** across all your merchants
- 🔧 **Customize payment flows** to match your requirements
- 🌍 **Expand to new markets** with Vinr's infrastructure
