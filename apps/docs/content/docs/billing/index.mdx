VINR Billing turns the one-time payment primitives in [Payments](/docs/payments) into recurring revenue. Model your pricing once, then run subscriptions, send invoices, meter usage, apply tax, and recover failed payments — all reconciled with your [settlements](/docs/operations/settlement) and exposed through the same API and webhooks.

## How it relates to Payments

Billing never moves money on its own — it _decides what to charge and when_, then hands the charge to Payments to collect on whatever rail the customer's stored method uses. A subscription renewal becomes an invoice; a finalized invoice becomes a [payment](/docs/payments/how-payments-work) against the customer's [stored method](/docs/payments/customers); the resulting funds land in your [balance](/docs/operations/balances) and pay out via [settlement](/docs/operations/settlement).

<Callout type="info">
Because Billing reuses the Payments customer and stored-method objects, a card saved during checkout is immediately usable for subscriptions — there's no second integration to build.
</Callout>

## Key objects

| Object | Prefix | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Product | `prod_` | A thing you sell (e.g. "Pro plan"). |
| Price | `price_` | An amount + billing behavior attached to a product. |
| Subscription | `sub_` | A recurring relationship between a customer and one or more prices. |
| Invoice | `inv_` | An itemized statement VINR collects payment against. |
| Usage record | `mbu_` | Reported consumption against a metered price. |

## Choose your billing model

<Steps>

### Fixed recurring (SaaS plans)

A flat amount on a fixed cycle. Create a recurring `price`, attach it to a `subscription`, and VINR invoices automatically each period. Start with [Create a subscription](/docs/guides/create-a-subscription).

### Usage-based / metered

Bill for what customers consume. Define a metered price, report [usage records](/docs/billing/usage-based-billing), and VINR aggregates and invoices at period end. See [Bill for usage](/docs/guides/bill-for-usage).

### One-off invoices

Bill a customer directly without a subscription — useful for services or custom deals. See [Send an invoice](/docs/guides/send-an-invoice).

</Steps>
