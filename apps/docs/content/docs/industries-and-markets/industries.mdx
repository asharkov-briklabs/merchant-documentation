Different industries have different payment needs. Use this guide to identify the VINR capabilities most relevant to your business, then follow the linked documentation to enable them.

## Retail and e-commerce

Online and brick-and-mortar retailers need a broad payment stack that handles card-not-present fraud, supports local payment methods, and enables seamless omnichannel flows.

| Capability | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [Card payments](/docs/payments/payment-methods) | Accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and local schemes |
| [3D Secure](/docs/payments/3d-secure) | Reduce fraud liability on card-not-present transactions |
| [Tokenization](/docs/payments/tokenization) | Store cards securely for repeat purchases |
| [Recurring payments](/docs/payments/recurring-payments) | Subscriptions, installment billing |
| [Refunds](/docs/payments/refunds) | Full and partial refunds with audit trail |
| [In-person payments](/docs/payments/in-person) | Unified online + in-store acceptance |
| [Disputes](/docs/payments/disputes) | Chargeback response tooling |
| [Reporting](/docs/operations/reporting) | Reconcile across channels |

## Travel and hospitality

Travel merchants deal with high-value transactions, multi-currency bookings, and pre-authorizations that settle days or weeks after capture.

| Capability | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [Authorization holds](/docs/payments/authorization-adjustment) | Pre-auth for hotel check-in, car rental |
| [Multi-currency payments](/docs/payments/payment-methods) | Accept bookings in the traveler's local currency |
| [Tokenization](/docs/payments/tokenization) | Secure card storage across the booking lifecycle |
| [3D Secure](/docs/payments/3d-secure) | Comply with SCA requirements in regulated markets |
| [Partial authorization](/docs/payments/partial-authorization) | Handle split-tender bookings |
| [Refunds](/docs/payments/refunds) | Cancellation and partial refund flows |
| [Reporting](/docs/operations/reporting) | Reconcile revenue across booking channels |

## Restaurant and food & beverage

Quick-service and full-service restaurants need fast terminal throughput, tip handling, and split-check support.

| Capability | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [In-person payments](/docs/payments/in-person) | Terminal-based card acceptance |
| [Tap to Pay](/docs/payments/in-person/tap-to-pay) | Accept contactless on a mobile device |
| [Terminal hardware](/docs/payments/in-person/terminals) | Countertop, handheld, and kiosk terminals |
| [Partial payments](/docs/payments/partial-payments) | Split checks across multiple payment methods |
| [Tokenization](/docs/payments/tokenization) | Stored cards for loyalty and repeat orders |
| [Refunds](/docs/payments/refunds) | Void and refund at POS |

## Healthcare

Healthcare providers collect patient payments subject to strict data-handling and billing regulations. Card-on-file and deferred billing are common patterns.

| Capability | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [Tokenization](/docs/payments/tokenization) | Securely store payment credentials for future billing |
| [Recurring payments](/docs/payments/recurring-payments) | Installment plans and recurring copays |
| [Partial authorization](/docs/payments/partial-authorization) | Collect what's available; bill the remainder later |
| [Refunds](/docs/payments/refunds) | Overpayment and insurance-adjustment refunds |
| [Compliance](/docs/compliance) | PCI DSS scope guidance |
| [Reporting](/docs/operations/reporting) | Patient payment reconciliation |

## Platforms and marketplaces

Platforms that facilitate payments on behalf of sub-merchants need split settlement, onboarding, and compliance tooling.

| Capability | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [Payouts](/docs/operations/payouts) | Route funds to sellers and service providers |
| [Settlement](/docs/operations/settlement) | Configure split settlement and payout schedules |
| [Compliance](/docs/compliance) | KYC/KYB for sub-merchant onboarding |
| [Webhooks](/docs/integration/webhooks) | Real-time payment event delivery to your platform |
| [Reporting](/docs/operations/reporting) | Aggregate and per-seller financial reporting |
| [Disputes](/docs/payments/disputes) | Manage chargebacks across your seller base |

## Subscription and SaaS businesses

Software and media businesses rely on reliable recurring billing, smart retry logic, and failed-payment recovery.

| Capability | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [Recurring payments](/docs/payments/recurring-payments) | Fixed and usage-based billing cycles |
| [Subscriptions](/docs/billing/subscriptions) | Plan management, upgrades, downgrades |
| [Invoices](/docs/billing/invoices) | Automated invoice generation and delivery |
| [Usage-based billing](/docs/billing/usage-based-billing) | Meter-based charges |
| [Tokenization](/docs/payments/tokenization) | Card-on-file without re-prompting customers |
| [3D Secure](/docs/payments/3d-secure) | SCA-exempt initial charges with MIT for renewals |
| [Reporting](/docs/operations/reporting) | MRR and churn cohort reconciliation |
