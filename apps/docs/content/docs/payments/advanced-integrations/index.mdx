This section covers integration patterns beyond the standard payment flow — for teams that need full control over checkout UI, complex authorization patterns, or enterprise-grade processor routing. Each topic is independently usable; pick the ones that apply to your business.

Whether you are building a bespoke checkout UI, handling hotel-style extended holds, charging customers asynchronously in the background, or routing transactions across multiple acquirers, the guides here give you the primitives and patterns to do it correctly.

## In this section

| Page | What it covers |
|------|----------------|
| [Custom payment flows](/docs/payments/advanced-integrations/custom-payment-flows) | Full control over UI and logic via the PaymentIntents state machine and VINR Elements |
| [Flexible acquiring](/docs/payments/advanced-integrations/flexible-acquiring) | Extended auth, incremental auth, multicapture, and overcapture for hospitality and marketplace verticals |
| [Off-session payments](/docs/payments/advanced-integrations/off-session-payments) | Charge saved methods server-side without the customer present |
| [Multiprocessor orchestration](/docs/payments/advanced-integrations/multiprocessor-orchestration) | Route across acquirers to optimize acceptance rate, cost, and resilience |
