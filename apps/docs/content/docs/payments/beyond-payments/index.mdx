VINR extends beyond traditional payment rails into two emerging areas reshaping commerce: crypto infrastructure and AI-native purchasing patterns. These capabilities are production-ready for early adopters and evolving rapidly.

Whether you're looking to accept stablecoins at checkout, disburse funds cross-border without slow fiat corridors, or position your platform for a world where AI agents make purchases on behalf of users — these features are live and available to qualifying merchants today.

## Crypto

Accept stablecoin payments and settle in fiat, pay out to users in stablecoin, or embed a fiat-to-crypto onramp directly in your product. VINR handles the custody, conversion, and compliance surface so you don't have to.

[Explore crypto capabilities](/docs/payments/beyond-payments/crypto)

## Agentic commerce

Enable AI agents to browse your catalog, add items to a cart, and complete purchases on behalf of your users — all without a human manually operating a browser. VINR Checkout links are natively agent-traversable, and the Payments API supports machine-initiated transactions scoped to pre-authorized agent wallets.

[Explore agentic commerce](/docs/payments/beyond-payments/agentic-commerce)
