This page is a feature reference for VINR terminals. All features described here are available on the supported terminal lineup — Nexgo N92, Nexgo N86Pro, Nexgo CT20, Nexgo CT20P, and Ciontek CM30 — but every feature must be enabled on your account before it becomes active. Contact your VINR account manager or toggle the feature in the Dashboard under Settings → Payment processing.

## Tipping

Tipping lets customers add a gratuity at the terminal before the payment is captured. You can offer percentage-based options, a fixed amount prompt, or both.

**Modes:**

- `percentage` — the terminal displays two or three pre-set percentage buttons (e.g. 15 %, 18 %, 20 %) and a custom entry.
- `amount` — the terminal displays a number pad and the customer keys in a currency amount.

Pass `tipConfig` when creating the terminal payment. The tip the customer selects is returned as `tipAmount` on the payment response alongside `amount` (the base) and `total` (base + tip).

```typescript
import { Vinr } from '@vinr/sdk';

const vinr = new Vinr({ secretKey: process.env.VINR_SECRET_KEY });

const terminalPayment = await vinr.terminal.payments.create({
  terminalId: 'term_01HZ5QXYZ',
  amount: 4500,
  currency: 'USD',
  reference: 'order_1042',
  tipConfig: {
    mode: 'percentage',
    percentages: [15, 18, 20],
    allowCustom: true,
  },
});
```

The response carries `tipAmount` once the customer confirms:

```typescript
console.log(terminalPayment.tipAmount);
console.log(terminalPayment.total);
```

## Surcharging

When surcharging is active, the terminal presents the surcharge amount to the customer on a separate screen before they tap or insert their card. The customer must confirm before the payment proceeds. See [Surcharging](/docs/payments/surcharging) for full configuration details, jurisdiction restrictions, and rate options.

```typescript
const terminalPayment = await vinr.terminal.payments.create({
  terminalId: 'term_01HZ5QXYZ',
  amount: 8000,
  currency: 'USD',
  reference: 'order_2201',
  surchargeConfig: {
    mode: 'auto',
  },
});
```

`surchargeAmount` is returned on the terminal payment object alongside the base `amount`.

**Availability:** all terminals. Requires surcharging to be enabled in Dashboard → Settings → Payment processing → Surcharging.

<Callout type="warn">
Surcharging is prohibited on consumer cards in the EU and UK and is restricted in several US states. Verify applicable regulations with qualified legal counsel before enabling this feature in any market.
</Callout>

## Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC)

Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) detects when a customer presents a foreign-issued card and offers to convert the transaction total to the cardholder's home currency at the point of sale. The customer chooses between the local currency (your settlement currency) or their home currency. Regardless of the customer's choice, you always settle in your local currency — the exchange-rate risk sits with the DCC provider.

Enable DCC in Dashboard → Settings → Payment processing → Dynamic Currency Conversion. No code change is required; the terminal handles the offer flow automatically when an eligible foreign card is detected.

**Availability:** all terminals. DCC requires a DCC agreement with VINR and is subject to card scheme rules. Not available in all markets.

<Callout type="info">
DCC is scheme-regulated. Mastercard and Visa have specific disclosure requirements for DCC offers. VINR's certified terminal software satisfies these requirements out of the box — do not attempt to suppress or modify the DCC screens.
</Callout>

## Cashback

Cashback lets a customer receive physical cash from the merchant at the point of sale as part of a debit card transaction. The cashback amount is added to the payment total; the merchant dispenses the cash and is reimbursed through settlement.

Pass `cashback` when creating the terminal payment. The terminal prompts the customer to select or enter a cashback amount up to `maxAmount`.

```typescript
const terminalPayment = await vinr.terminal.payments.create({
  terminalId: 'term_01HZ5QXYZ',
  amount: 3200,
  currency: 'GBP',
  reference: 'order_5510',
  cashback: {
    maxAmount: 5000,
  },
});
```

**Availability:** all terminals. Debit cards only; credit cards are excluded in all supported markets. Cashback must be enabled at account level and is subject to per-transaction limits set in the Dashboard.

## MOTO

Mail Order / Telephone Order (MOTO) payments are card-not-present transactions initiated at the terminal. A staff member keys in the customer's card number, expiry, and CVV when the customer is not physically present — for example, when taking a payment over the phone.

Set `motoPayment: true` on the terminal payment to flag the transaction as MOTO. The terminal presents a manual key-entry screen regardless of whether a card is presented.

```typescript
const terminalPayment = await vinr.terminal.payments.create({
  terminalId: 'term_01HZ5QXYZ',
  amount: 15000,
  currency: 'USD',
  reference: 'order_7730',
  motoPayment: true,
});
```

<Callout type="warn">
MOTO payments do not qualify for standard chargeback protection. Because the cardholder is not present to authenticate, disputes are more likely to succeed in the cardholder's favour. Use MOTO only when no other payment method is available and document customer authorisation for your records. MOTO transactions also place your business under a higher PCI DSS scope (SAQ C or SAQ D depending on your environment) — review your compliance posture before enabling this feature.
</Callout>

**Availability:** all terminals. Requires the MOTO feature to be enabled at account level. Staff permissions for MOTO key entry are configured per terminal role in Dashboard → Terminals → Roles.

## Manual key entry

Manual key entry is a fallback method that allows a staff member to type in card details directly on the terminal when both contactless and chip entry fail. It is distinct from MOTO: the customer is physically present but the card cannot be read electronically.

Manual key entry is enabled per terminal role in Dashboard → Terminals → Roles. A staff PIN or manager override is required before the terminal allows key entry.

<Callout type="warn">
Manual key entry is a last resort and should be used only when chip and contactless have both genuinely failed. It bypasses EMV security guarantees and produces a card-not-present liability shift. Train staff not to default to key entry as a convenience measure.
</Callout>

**Availability:** all terminals. Staff permission required.

## Offline payments

When a terminal loses its network connection, offline mode queues approved transactions locally and syncs them to the VINR platform when connectivity is restored. This prevents the checkout experience from failing completely during brief outages.

Configure offline mode per terminal in Dashboard → Terminals → select terminal → Advanced → Offline payments, or pass `offlineMode` in the terminal payment create call to override the terminal default for a single transaction.

```typescript
const terminalPayment = await vinr.terminal.payments.create({
  terminalId: 'term_01HZ5QXYZ',
  amount: 2000,
  currency: 'USD',
  reference: 'order_9901',
  offlineMode: 'allow',
});
```

<TypeTable
  type={{
    offlineMode: {
      description: "Controls offline queuing for this payment. allow enables offline queuing; deny forces the terminal to decline if offline; terminal_default defers to the terminal configuration.",
      type: '"allow" | "deny" | "terminal_default"',
      default: '"terminal_default"',
    },
  }}
/>

<Callout type="warn">
Offline payments carry a risk of decline on sync. The payment is approved locally based on floor limits and card BIN data cached on the terminal, but final authorisation happens when the terminal reconnects. If the issuer declines on sync — for example due to insufficient funds or a card being reported lost — you will receive a `payment.failed` webhook and must treat the transaction as unsuccessful. Offline mode should not be used as a substitute for a reliable network connection.
</Callout>

**Availability:** Nexgo N92, Nexgo N86Pro, Nexgo CT20, Nexgo CT20P. The Ciontek CM30 does not support offline queuing. Offline limits and queue size are configurable per terminal.

## Installments

Installments at the terminal let customers split the purchase amount into equal monthly payments. Pass `installments.enabled: true` on the terminal payment create call. The terminal presents eligible plan options after the card is read, provided the card issuer participates in an installment programme.

```typescript
const terminalPayment = await vinr.terminal.payments.create({
  terminalId: 'term_01HZ5QXYZ',
  amount: 60000,
  currency: 'EUR',
  reference: 'order_3310',
  installments: {
    enabled: true,
  },
});
```

You receive the full purchase amount at settlement regardless of the instalment schedule the customer selects. For full eligibility details, market availability, and supported card brands, see [Installments](/docs/payments/installments).

**Availability:** all terminals. Subject to issuer participation and market availability. Installments must be enabled at account level.

## Gift cards

VINR-managed gift cards can be redeemed at the terminal. The customer presents their gift card (physical card, virtual card number, or QR code depending on your gift card configuration) and the terminal checks the balance against VINR's gift card service.

If the gift card balance covers the full transaction amount, the payment completes on the gift card. If the balance is insufficient, the terminal automatically initiates a split tender: the gift card covers the available balance and the customer is prompted to present a second payment method for the remainder. See [Partial payments](/docs/payments/partial-payments) for how split-tender totals are tracked on your backend.

```typescript
const terminalPayment = await vinr.terminal.payments.create({
  terminalId: 'term_01HZ5QXYZ',
  amount: 9500,
  currency: 'USD',
  reference: 'order_4420',
  paymentMethodTypes: ['gift_card', 'card'],
});
```

Including `gift_card` first in `paymentMethodTypes` signals to the terminal that it should offer gift card redemption as the primary tender, falling back to card if the gift card is absent or has zero balance.

**Availability:** all terminals. Requires the VINR gift card programme to be configured for your account.

<Accordion title="Advanced — combined features, custom feature profiles, and scheme availability">

**Combining features**

Multiple features can be active on a single terminal payment. For example, tipping and surcharging can both be enabled simultaneously; the terminal applies the surcharge to the base amount first, then presents the tip prompt on the surcharged total. DCC and installments can also coexist: if the customer selects DCC, installment plans are shown in the converted home currency. MOTO and tipping cannot be combined — key-entry flows do not present a tip prompt.

**Custom feature profiles per location**

If you operate multiple locations with different feature requirements — for example, tipping enabled at restaurant locations but not retail — you can create feature profiles in Dashboard → Settings → Payment processing → Feature profiles and assign a profile to each terminal. Profiles override the account-level defaults for every terminal they are assigned to. API overrides in the payment create call take precedence over both the profile and the account default.

**Availability by card scheme**

| Feature | Visa | Mastercard | Amex | Discover | UnionPay |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tipping | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Surcharging | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| DCC | Yes | Yes | No | No | Yes |
| Cashback | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| MOTO | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Offline payments | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Installments | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Gift cards | n/a | n/a | n/a | n/a | n/a |

Scheme availability is subject to change as VINR expands programme coverage. Gift cards are a VINR-native tender and are independent of card scheme rules.
