Online payments covers every digital payment flow VINR supports: from a simple one-shot card charge through to complex deferred-capture, multi-currency, and installment scenarios.

## Pages in this section

- **[Authorize & capture](/docs/payments/authorize-and-capture)** — Separate authorization from capture for delayed fulfilment, deposits, and orders whose final amount isn't known at checkout.
- **[Authorization adjustments](/docs/payments/authorization-adjustment)** — Increment or decrement an open authorization before capture — useful for hotels, car rentals, and metered services.
- **[Payment links](/docs/payments/payment-links)** — Generate a shareable URL that opens a hosted checkout page — no integration code required.
- **[Recurring payments](/docs/payments/recurring-payments)** — Store credentials and charge customers on a schedule or on demand, including MIT and CIT flows.
- **[Multicurrency & FX](/docs/payments/multicurrency)** — Accept payments in the cardholder's currency, present dynamic currency conversion, and settle in your preferred currency.
- **[Installments](/docs/payments/installments)** — Split a single purchase into equal monthly payments, supported on eligible cards in supported markets.
- **[Partial authorization](/docs/payments/partial-authorization)** — Handle issuer responses that approve only part of the requested amount, common for prepaid and debit cards.
- **[Partial payments](/docs/payments/partial-payments)** — Let customers split a single order across multiple payment methods or tender types.
- **[Donations](/docs/payments/donations)** — Accept open-amount or fixed-tier charitable contributions with Gift Aid eligibility support.
