<Callout type="warn">
**Dynamic price and shipping updates are not currently supported.** Google Pay™'s `onPaymentDataChanged` callback — used with the `SHIPPING_ADDRESS`, `SHIPPING_OPTION`, and `OFFER` `callbackIntents` for real-time shipping cost, tax, or promo-code recalculation inside the Google Pay sheet — is **not implemented** in VINR's Google Pay integration. Do not configure `onPaymentDataChanged` expecting VINR to process it. (VINR does use the separate `onPaymentAuthorized` callback internally to acknowledge the payment result before the sheet closes — that is handled for you and requires no configuration; see [VINR's current behavior](#vinrs-current-behavior).) If in-sheet recalculation is a requirement for your integration, contact your VINR account manager before you build against it.
</Callout>

## What this feature normally does

Google's Google Pay API supports registering callbacks in `PaymentsClient` so a merchant can recalculate the order total, shipping options, or applied offers while the payment sheet is still open — before the customer authenticates. This is documented by Google as three related capabilities:

- **Authorize Payments** (`onPaymentAuthorized`) — acknowledge authorization status before the sheet closes. *(VINR uses this one internally — it is not something you configure. The two below are the ones that are not supported.)*
- **Dynamic price updates** (`onPaymentDataChanged` with `SHIPPING_ADDRESS` / `SHIPPING_OPTION`) — recompute totals as the customer picks a shipping address or option.
- **Promo codes / offers** (`onPaymentDataChanged` with `OFFER`) — validate a redemption code and adjust the total.

See Google's own [Web integration tutorial](https://developers.google.com/pay/api/web/guides/tutorial) for the full mechanics of these callbacks (`paymentDataCallbacks`, `callbackIntents`, `PaymentDataRequestUpdate`, `PaymentDataError`).

## VINR's current behavior

VINR's Google Pay integration uses a fixed-total flow: the payment sheet is configured with a final `transactionInfo.totalPrice`, the customer authenticates, and the resulting encrypted token is submitted to VINR. VINR **does** register `onPaymentAuthorized` internally (in Hosted Checkout and Payment Links) to acknowledge the authorization result before the sheet closes — this is handled for you and needs no configuration. VINR does **not** register `onPaymentDataChanged`, so real-time price, shipping, and offer recalculation inside the sheet is not available, and the hosted surfaces do not expose `callbackIntents` for you to configure.

Practical implications:

- **Show the final price before the sheet opens.** Resolve shipping cost, tax, and any discounts in your own checkout UI first, and pass the confirmed total into the Google Pay request. Do not rely on the payment sheet to recalculate the total for you.
- **Shipping selection happens outside the Google Pay sheet.** Collect the shipping method the same way you would for a card payment, before the customer taps the Google Pay button.

## If you need this

If your business requires in-sheet recalculation (for example, address-dependent shipping tiers or promo codes redeemed at the moment of payment), contact your VINR account manager to discuss options. Do not implement Google's callback intents against a VINR Direct API integration and assume the resulting token will be handled correctly — this path is unconfirmed and untested with VINR's backend.
