title: Go-live checklist
description: Verify your Google Pay integration before accepting live payments on VINR.

lastUpdated: "2026-06-15"
---

Use this checklist before switching your integration to the `PRODUCTION` environment. Walk through each section — missing any item is a common reason for Google to reject a merchant registration or for live payments to silently fail.

---

## 1. Account and registration

- [ ] **Google Pay enabled in Dashboard.** In **Settings → Payment methods**, confirm Google Pay is toggled on for your VINR account. Required before Google Pay tokens are accepted in production.
- [ ] **Google merchant registration complete.** Register your integration in the [Google Pay & Wallet Console](https://pay.google.com/business/console) with integration type **Gateway**, gateway `vinr`, and your VINR merchant ID. Wait for Google's approval email — typically 1–2 business days. You cannot accept live payments until registration is approved.
- [ ] **Production domains allowlisted.** Add every domain that will display the Google Pay button in the [Google Pay & Wallet Console](https://pay.google.com/business/console) under **Domain allowlist**. An unregistered domain causes `isReadyToPay()` to return `false` silently in production — the button will not appear. See [Test & go live — troubleshooting](/docs/payments/payment-methods/add-payment-methods/wallets/google-pay/test-and-go-live#troubleshooting).
- [ ] **Merchant IDs populated in your integration.** Confirm both IDs are set correctly in your `paymentDataRequest`: `merchantInfo.merchantId` (your Google merchant ID from the Console) and `tokenizationSpecification.parameters.gatewayMerchantId` (your VINR merchant ID). Mixing these up is the most common cause of `invalid_token` errors in production.

---

## 2. Authentication methods and card networks

- [ ] **`allowedAuthMethods` confirmed with VINR.** Check with your VINR account manager which methods are enabled — `CRYPTOGRAM_3DS`, `PAN_ONLY`, or both. Your `allowedAuthMethods` array must match exactly. A mismatch causes a runtime error when the payment sheet loads. See [Authentication methods](/docs/payments/payment-methods/add-payment-methods/wallets/google-pay/authentication-methods).
- [ ] **`allowedCardNetworks` confirmed with VINR.** Set `allowedCardNetworks` to only the networks enabled on your VINR account (`MASTERCARD` and/or `VISA`). Specifying an unsupported network — for example Amex or Discover — causes token decryption failures.

---

## 3. Environment switch

- [ ] **`PaymentsClient` set to `PRODUCTION`.** For direct API integrations, update the `PaymentsClient` constructor to `environment: 'PRODUCTION'`. For Elements and hosted checkout, no code change is needed — switch to your production publishable key and VINR handles the environment automatically.

---

## 4. Button and brand compliance

- [ ] **Button rendered via approved API or element.** The Google Pay button must be rendered using `googlePayClient.createButton()` or the VINR `GooglePayElement`. Do not create a custom button, image, or HTML substitute — this will fail Google's integration review.
- [ ] **Button only shown when `isReadyToPay` returns `true`.** The button must be hidden (not disabled) when `isReadyToPay()` returns `false`. Displaying it unconditionally is a brand guideline violation.
- [ ] **Button receives visual parity with other payment methods.** The Google Pay button must receive equal or greater prominence compared to other third-party payment methods — not smaller, below the fold, or styled to appear secondary. See [Button guidelines](/docs/payments/payment-methods/add-payment-methods/wallets/google-pay/button-guidelines).
- [ ] **Button color matches the background.** Use the black button on light backgrounds and the white button on dark backgrounds. The white button requires a visible border — do not render it without a border on a white background.

---

## 5. Checkout experience

- [ ] **`loadPaymentData()` called synchronously in the click handler.** Call `loadPaymentData()` directly inside the button's click handler — not after an `await` or inside a `.then()`. Deferring the call breaks user-activation and causes pop-up blockers to intercept the payment sheet in most browsers. See [Set up Google Pay](/docs/payments/payment-methods/add-payment-methods/wallets/google-pay/setup).
- [ ] **Final price confirmed before processing.** The customer must see the final order total before the payment is submitted. If the amount changes based on data returned from the Google Pay sheet (for example, shipping cost derived from the shipping address), show a confirmation step with the final price before calling your server-side payment creation endpoint.
- [ ] **`PAN_ONLY` `requires_action` handled.** PAN_ONLY transactions enter `requires_action` when VINR's 3DS step-up triggers. Elements and hosted checkout handle this automatically. For direct API integrations, confirm your server handles `requires_action` identically to any 3DS card payment. See [Authentication methods](/docs/payments/payment-methods/add-payment-methods/wallets/google-pay/authentication-methods) and [Strong Customer Authentication](/docs/payments/strong-customer-authentication).

---

## 6. Error handling

- [ ] **Customer cancellation handled silently.** When the customer dismisses the payment sheet, the Promise rejects with `statusCode: 'CANCELED'`. Catch this and do not show an error message — let the customer retry or choose another payment method.
- [ ] **Declines surface a clear message with retry option.** On a decline, display the reason clearly and offer the customer a way to retry or choose a different payment method. Do not leave the checkout in a broken or unresponsive state.

---

## 7. Webhook confirmation

- [ ] **Fulfillment triggered by webhook, not client-side redirect.** Order fulfillment — confirmation email, access provisioning, shipping — must be triggered by the `payment.completed` webhook received on your server, not by the client-side redirect. The customer's browser may close before the redirect fires. Confirm your webhook endpoint is live, reachable from the internet, and correctly verifying the VINR signature before going live.

---

## 8. Dynamic checkout updates (if applicable)

- [ ] **`callbackIntents` matches your callback implementations.** Every intent listed in `callbackIntents` must have a corresponding handler in `paymentDataCallbacks`. Declaring an intent without a handler causes the payment sheet to stall indefinitely.
- [ ] **All callbacks resolve promptly.** Add timeouts to any network calls made inside callbacks — a callback that hangs will freeze the payment sheet for the customer. See [Dynamic updates](/docs/payments/payment-methods/add-payment-methods/wallets/google-pay/dynamic-updates).

---

## 9. Final sign-off

- [ ] **End-to-end test completed in production.** Run a live transaction using a real card after switching to `PRODUCTION`. Confirm the payment appears in the VINR Dashboard and the fulfillment webhook fires correctly.
- [ ] **Google merchant registration status confirmed active.** Verify your registration status is **Approved** in the [Google Pay & Wallet Console](https://pay.google.com/business/console). The Google Pay button will not appear for unregistered or pending merchant IDs.
- [ ] **Google Pay mark added to checkout and marketing materials.** Inform customers you accept Google Pay using only the approved Google Pay mark. See [Button guidelines](/docs/payments/payment-methods/add-payment-methods/wallets/google-pay/button-guidelines).
