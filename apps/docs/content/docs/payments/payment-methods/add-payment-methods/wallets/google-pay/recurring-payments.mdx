<Callout type="warn">
**Unconfirmed — verify with VINR.** Whether, and how, a Google Pay™ payment method can be saved and charged off-session through VINR is not yet confirmed. There is no documented Google Pay-specific save/charge API for this today. Contact your VINR account manager before building a recurring or off-session Google Pay flow.
</Callout>

## Why this is different from a saved card

Google Pay does not have a merchant-token (MPAN) equivalent the way Apple Pay does. Each Google Pay payment sheet interaction produces either:

- a device-bound one-time cryptogram (`CRYPTOGRAM_3DS`), or
- a customer-authorized PAN-only credential (`PAN_ONLY`), routed through VINR's 3D Secure step-up.

Neither credential type is, by itself, a reusable "wallet on file" token — Google's API is designed around a fresh customer interaction with the payment sheet for each charge. Reusing a Google Pay result for a later, customer-absent charge depends entirely on how the underlying card data is captured and stored on the processor side, which is why this needs to be confirmed with VINR rather than assumed from Google's client-side documentation.

## What to check with VINR

Before relying on Google Pay for subscriptions or other off-session billing, confirm with your VINR account manager:

- Whether VINR currently supports saving a payment method sourced from a Google Pay transaction for later off-session use.
- If supported, which credential type (`PAN_ONLY` or `CRYPTOGRAM_3DS`) is eligible, and any authentication requirements for the first (on-session) transaction.
- How subsequent off-session charges should be initiated, and how failures or step-up requirements on those charges are surfaced back to you.
