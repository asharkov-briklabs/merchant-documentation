VINR is multi-rail: a single integration unlocks cards, bank transfers, stablecoins, and local methods. You enable methods once at the account level, and the same `payment` object works across all of them. This overview helps you decide which to turn on, where, and why.

## Methods at a glance

Each method rides a **rail** — the network that actually moves the money. The `payment` object and its lifecycle are identical regardless of rail; only cost, speed, and reversibility differ.

| Method | Rail | Settlement speed | Finality | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) | Cards | Instant auth, T+1–2 settle | Reversible (chargebacks) | Global default, recurring |
| SEPA Credit Transfer | Bank transfer | Minutes–1 day | Strong | EU B2B, high-value |
| SEPA Direct Debit | Bank transfer | 2–5 days | Reversible (8-week recall) | EU recurring |
| Faster Payments | Bank transfer | Seconds–minutes | Strong | UK payouts & pay-ins |
| iDEAL | Local | Minutes | Strong | Netherlands |
| Blik | Local | Instant | Strong | Poland |
| USDC / EURC | Stablecoin | Minutes (on confirmation) | Final | Cross-border, crypto-native |

<Callout type="info">
The customer picks a method on the hosted checkout based on what you've enabled and their region. You never hard-code a single method — VINR shows the right options automatically.
</Callout>

## Choosing methods by region

Conversion is highest when local favourites are offered. A rough starting point:

| Region | Enable first | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Eurozone | Cards, SEPA, iDEAL, Blik | Bank rails dominate B2B; local methods lift consumer conversion |
| UK | Cards, Faster Payments | Instant bank rails are widely trusted |
| Cross-border / crypto | Cards, USDC, EURC | Stablecoins avoid FX spread and slow correspondent banking |
| Global fallback | Cards | Universally accepted; safe default everywhere |

<Callout type="warn">
Some methods are single-currency. iDEAL and SEPA settle in EUR only; Faster Payments in GBP. If you charge a customer in an unsupported currency, that method simply won't appear at checkout.
</Callout>

## Cost, speed & finality trade-offs

Three dimensions drive the choice:

- **Cost** — cards carry interchange plus scheme fees; bank transfers and stablecoins are typically cheaper per transaction. See [pricing & fees](/docs/operations/settlement).
- **Speed** — stablecoins and instant bank rails clear in minutes; card funds settle to your balance on a delay; direct debits take days.
- **Finality** — cards and SEPA Direct Debit are reversible (chargebacks, recalls). SEPA Credit Transfer, Faster Payments, and confirmed stablecoin transfers are effectively final, which lowers your dispute exposure.

For high-value or fraud-sensitive orders, prefer a final rail. For frictionless consumer checkout, cards and local methods win on conversion.

## Enabling methods

Enable methods in the Dashboard under **Settings → Payment methods**, or programmatically. Newly enabled methods apply to all future payments without code changes.

```typescript
import { Vinr } from '@vinr/sdk';

const vinr = new Vinr({ secretKey: process.env.VINR_SECRET_KEY });

// Turn on the methods this account should offer
await vinr.paymentMethods.update({
  enabled: ['card', 'sepa_credit_transfer', 'ideal', 'usdc'],
});

// Inspect what's currently active and any per-method constraints
const config = await vinr.paymentMethods.list();
for (const m of config.data) {
  console.log(m.method, m.status, m.currencies.join(','));
  // → "ideal" "active" "EUR"
}
```

To restrict the methods shown on a single checkout — for example, only bank rails for a B2B invoice — pass `paymentMethods` when creating the payment:

```typescript
const payment = await vinr.payments.create({
  amount: 250000,                 // €2,500.00
  currency: 'EUR',
  description: 'Invoice #INV-204',
  returnUrl: 'https://yoursite.com/thanks',
  paymentMethods: ['sepa_credit_transfer', 'card'],
});
```

<Callout type="info">
Test each method in sandbox before going live. Use card `4242 4242 4242 4242` for a success, `4000 0000 0000 0002` for a decline, and `4000 0000 0000 3220` to exercise 3DS. Bank and stablecoin methods auto-confirm in sandbox.
</Callout>

## Per-method guides

Each rail has its own quirks — mandates for direct debit, network confirmations for stablecoins, redirect flows for local methods. Dive into the dedicated guides:

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Cards">
    Global default. Supports authorize-and-capture, 3DS/SCA, and saved cards for recurring billing. See [authorize & capture](/docs/payments/authorize-and-capture).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bank transfers (SEPA, Faster Payments)">
    Lower cost, strong finality for credit transfers. Direct debit requires a stored mandate and can be recalled within the scheme window.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stablecoins (USDC, EURC)">
    Funds are final once the required network confirmations land. No FX spread for cross-border. Settled to your VINR balance like any other rail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Local methods (iDEAL, Blik)">
    Redirect-based; the customer authenticates in their banking app. Single-currency and region-specific, but convert exceptionally well in-market.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
