Link is VINR's accelerated checkout network. Returning Link customers see their saved payment method pre-filled and can confirm in a single tap — no re-entering details.

## How it works

1. The customer enters their email on your checkout form.
2. If they have a Link account, VINR sends a one-time code to their phone.
3. After verification, their saved card or bank account is pre-populated in the Payment Element.
4. They confirm — done in one tap.

First-time Link users are enrolled automatically when they save a payment method through any Link-enabled checkout. Their credentials follow them to every merchant using Link.

## Integration

Link activates automatically inside the Payment Element — no additional configuration required. Mount the element as you normally would and Link surfaces for eligible customers without any extra code.

```ts
// Client: mount the Payment Element — Link is included automatically
import { loadVinr } from '@vinr/sdk';

const vinr = await loadVinr('pk_live_...');
const elements = vinr.elements({ clientSecret });

const paymentElement = elements.create('payment');
paymentElement.mount('#payment-element');
```

Link also works in VINR Checkout and Payment Links with zero setup — it is on by default for all hosted payment surfaces.

## Instant Bank Payments

Customers who connect a bank account to their Link account unlock Instant Bank Payments — a direct bank rail with lower interchange than cards. From your side, settlement works exactly the same as a card payment; you receive fiat funds on your normal payout schedule. The customer simply opts into a faster, often cheaper path at the moment of checkout.

No additional integration is required to support Instant Bank Payments. If a customer has a linked bank account, the option appears automatically within Link.
