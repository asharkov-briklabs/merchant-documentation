Local methods are region-specific ways to pay that customers trust over cards in their home market — iDEAL in the Netherlands, Blik in Poland, Bancontact in Belgium, and more. Most are bank-redirect or app-confirmation flows: the customer approves the charge in their own banking app, which means high conversion and, for many of them, payments that are final the moment they confirm.

## Availability & currencies

Each method is tied to the customer's country and a single settlement currency. VINR surfaces only the methods that match the payment's currency and the customer's region, so you can pass `methods: ['auto']` and let the hosted page decide.

| Method | Region | Currency | Flow | Finality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| iDEAL | Netherlands | EUR | Bank redirect | Final |
| Blik | Poland | PLN | 6-digit code / app | Final |
| Bancontact | Belgium | EUR | Bank redirect / app | Final |
| EPS | Austria | EUR | Bank redirect | Final |
| Przelewy24 | Poland | PLN, EUR | Bank redirect | Final |
| Twint | Switzerland | CHF | App confirmation | Final |

<Callout type="info">
Local methods are single-currency: a customer paying with iDEAL must be charged in EUR, Blik in PLN. To present a local method to a shopper in another currency, convert the amount before creating the payment. See [multi-currency](/docs/payments/multi-currency).
</Callout>

## How the flow works

A local-method payment moves through the same `payment` object as any other rail, but always involves a redirect or app handoff:

<Steps>

### Create the payment

You create a `pending` payment scoped to one or more local methods and receive a `checkoutUrl`.

### Customer authenticates with their bank

The customer is redirected to (or confirms in) their bank or banking app. For Blik they enter a 6-digit code generated in-app.

### Bank confirms and redirects back

On approval the bank pushes the customer to your `returnUrl`. The payment moves to `completed`; most local methods are **final** at this point with no chargeback path.

### You fulfill on the webhook

Treat the `payment.completed` webhook — not the browser redirect — as the source of truth before releasing goods.

</Steps>

## Creating a payment

Restrict the payment to the methods you want, or pass `'auto'` to let VINR pick by region and currency. The hosted checkout renders the right bank picker or code entry.

```typescript
import { Vinr } from '@vinr/sdk';

const vinr = new Vinr({ secretKey: process.env.VINR_SECRET_KEY });

const payment = await vinr.payments.create({
  amount: 2500,                 // €25.00 in minor units
  currency: 'EUR',
  description: 'Order #4471',
  methods: ['ideal', 'bancontact'],
  returnUrl: 'https://yoursite.com/payment/complete',
  metadata: { orderId: '4471' },
});

// payment.id          → "pay_3Nf8x2a..."
// payment.status      → "pending"
// payment.checkoutUrl → hosted page with the iDEAL bank picker
```

Send the customer to `payment.checkoutUrl`. After the bank redirect, confirm fulfillment from the webhook rather than the return URL, which the customer may never reach:

```typescript
// POST /webhooks/vinr
const event = vinr.webhooks.verify(rawBody, req.headers['x-vinr-signature']);

if (event.type === 'payment.completed') {
  const payment = event.data;
  // payment.method → "ideal"
  await fulfillOrder(payment.metadata.orderId);
}
```

<Callout type="warn">
In sandbox, local-method redirects open a VINR test page where you choose **Approve** or **Fail** instead of a real bank. Test cards do not apply to these flows.
</Callout>

## Settlement & timing

Most local methods authorize and confirm in seconds, and the captured amount lands in your [balance](/docs/operations/balances) immediately — there is no separate capture step. From there, funds follow your normal [settlement](/docs/operations/settlement) schedule and are netted against fees and refunds during [reconciliation](/docs/operations/reconciliation).

A few methods (some bank-transfer-backed ones via Przelewy24) can stay `pending` for minutes while the underlying transfer clears. Always drive fulfillment from `payment.completed`, and surface `payment.expired` to the customer if they abandon the redirect.

## Refunds & reversals

Local methods support refunds through the standard refund API; the money is returned to the customer's originating bank account, typically within a few business days.

```typescript
const refund = await vinr.refunds.create({
  payment: 'pay_3Nf8x2a...',
  amount: 2500,                 // full refund; omit amount for full
  reason: 'requested_by_customer',
});

// refund.id     → "re_8Kd2p..."
// refund.status → "pending"
```

<Callout type="info">
Because most local methods are final, there are **no chargebacks** — a refund is the only way to return funds. Once a method completes you cannot reverse it any other way, so validate orders before fulfilling.
</Callout>

## Limitations

- **Single currency per method.** You cannot charge iDEAL in PLN or Blik in EUR.
- **No partial authorization or manual capture.** Local methods capture in full at confirmation.
- **No card-style 3DS step-up** — authentication happens inside the customer's bank.
- **Region-locked.** A method only appears for customers in its home country; presenting it elsewhere returns a `method_not_available` error.
