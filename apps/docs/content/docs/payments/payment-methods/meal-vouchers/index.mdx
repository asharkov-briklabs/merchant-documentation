Meal vouchers are regulated non-cash benefits issued by employers to employees for purchasing food at eligible businesses — restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, and food-related retail. They are a standard employee benefit in Bulgaria and across Europe.

VINR supports the same meal voucher providers active in France:

- **Sodexo** (Sodexo Pass)
- **Up** (formerly Ticket Restaurant, now part of Edenred group)
- **Swile**
- **Ticket Restaurant** (Edenred)

<Callout type="info">
In Bulgaria, meal vouchers are governed by the Bulgarian Labour Code and are a standard employee benefit. The same providers operating in France — Sodexo, Up, Swile, and Ticket Restaurant — are accepted through VINR.
</Callout>

## Eligible merchant categories

Meal vouchers can only be used at businesses in food-eligible merchant category codes (MCCs). Typical eligible categories include:

- Restaurants and cafés
- Grocery stores and supermarkets
- Bakeries and food specialty shops
- Catering services

Payments attempted at non-eligible MCCs are declined by the voucher provider. Ensure your VINR account is configured with the correct MCC for your business before enabling meal vouchers.

## Acceptance modes

| Mode | Providers supported | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| In-person (NFC card) | Sodexo, Up, Ticket Restaurant | Customer taps their physical voucher card at a VINR terminal |
| In-person (app) | Swile | Customer scans a QR code or uses NFC from the Swile app |
| Online | Sodexo, Up, Swile, Ticket Restaurant | Redirect-based flow — customer enters voucher details or authenticates in-app |
