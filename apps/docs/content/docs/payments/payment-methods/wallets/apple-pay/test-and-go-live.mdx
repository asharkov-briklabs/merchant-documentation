## Testing

Apple Pay in the VINR sandbox environment simulates the payment sheet without processing a real transaction. Use a device enrolled in the Apple Pay Sandbox (configured in Xcode or via an Apple Developer account) and any test card number from the VINR sandbox card list.

In the hosted checkout, Apple Pay appears automatically for eligible devices when sandbox mode is active.
