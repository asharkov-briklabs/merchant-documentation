A capture can fail in two distinct phases. Failures caught immediately return a `4xx` response with an `error` object — fix the request and resubmit. Failures that occur after submission are reported asynchronously via a `payment.capture.failed` webhook with a `reason` field.

This page covers the asynchronous failures — cases where VINR accepted the capture request (`status: "pending"`) but the card scheme or issuing bank later rejected it.

<Callout type="info">
The same failure patterns apply to refunds. See [Refund failure reasons](/docs/payments/payment-operations/refund/failure-reasons).
</Callout>

## Failure scenarios

### Technical issue

**Reason:** `technical_issue`

A transient problem occurred at the card scheme. VINR will automatically attempt to re-capture. If it succeeds you receive a `payment.capture.succeeded` event with `reason: "recaptured"` — no action needed.

If the re-capture does not succeed within 10 days, contact [VINR support](https://vinr.com/support).

### Customer cancelled a recurring payment

**Reason:** `customer_cancelled_recurring`

Applies to subscriptions and stored-credential transactions. The customer instructed their bank to block further charges from your merchant ID before you captured the payment. VINR cannot override this.

Reach out to the customer to update their payment method or reinstate the mandate, then create a new payment.

### Incorrect request

**Reason:** `transaction_not_found` · `account_mismatch`

Causes:
- The `paymentId` does not exist, is already fully captured, or belongs to a different account.
- The capture currency does not match the authorized currency.

Verify the `paymentId` and currency, then resubmit.

### Account configuration error

**Reason:** `operation_not_available`

The payment method or capture type requires a capability that has not been enabled on your account. Contact [VINR support](https://vinr.com/support) to enable it.

### Capture expired

**Reason:** `authorization_expired`

The authorization window closed before the capture was submitted. Authorization validity depends on the card scheme and the issuing bank — typically up to 7 days for most cards, shorter for some issuers.

You can re-authorize the card from **Dashboard → Payments → {payment} → Re-authorize**, or ask the customer to complete a new payment.

### Cannot be re-captured

**Reason:** `already_reversed` · `insufficient_balance`

The payment was already fully refunded, reversed, or cancelled. There is no remaining balance. A new payment is required.

## Quick reference

| Reason | Your action |
|---|---|
| `technical_issue` | None — VINR retries automatically. Escalate after 10 days. |
| `customer_cancelled_recurring` | Contact customer; collect a new payment method. |
| `transaction_not_found` / `account_mismatch` | Verify `paymentId` and currency; resubmit. |
| `operation_not_available` | Contact VINR support to enable the capability. |
| `authorization_expired` | Re-authorize via Dashboard or collect a new payment. |
| `already_reversed` / `insufficient_balance` | Collect a new payment. |
