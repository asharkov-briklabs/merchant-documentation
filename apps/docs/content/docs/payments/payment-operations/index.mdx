Running payments in production means more than processing individual transactions. The operational layer covers the tools and practices you need to understand payment performance, keep cash flowing predictably, and meet compliance obligations without surprises.

This section covers modifying payments after authorization, observability, cash flow management, and compliance. For fraud prevention, see [Fraud prevention](/docs/payments/fraud-prevention).

## Modify a payment

- **[Capture](/docs/payments/payment-operations/capture)** — Control when authorized funds settle: immediately, on a delay, or manually on fulfilment.
- **[Cancel](/docs/payments/payment-operations/cancel)** — Void an authorization before capture to release the hold immediately.
- **[Refund](/docs/payments/payment-operations/refund)** — Return captured funds fully or partially after settlement.
- **[Reverse](/docs/payments/payment-operations/reverse)** — Undo a captured payment before it settles — faster than a refund.
- **[Authorization Adjustment](/docs/payments/payment-operations/authorization-adjustment)** — Increase or decrease a pre-authorized amount before capturing.
