Running payments in production means more than processing individual transactions. The operational layer covers the tools and practices you need to understand payment performance, keep cash flowing predictably, and meet compliance obligations without surprises.

This section covers observability — acceptance rates, authentication performance, and optimization signals — alongside the practical mechanics of balances and settlement timing, compliance obligations, decline diagnosis, and automated receipts. For dispute handling, refund workflows, and fraud prevention, see [Disputes](/docs/payments/disputes), [Refunds](/docs/payments/refunds), and [Fraud prevention](/docs/payments/fraud-prevention).
