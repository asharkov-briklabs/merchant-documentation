## Overview

The analytics dashboard provides a real-time view of your payment performance.

## Dashboard Sections

### Overview

High-level metrics including:
- Total revenue
- Transaction count
- Success rate
- Active customers

### Revenue Chart

Visual representation of revenue over time with:
- Daily, weekly, or monthly views
- Comparison to previous periods
- Trend indicators

### Payment Methods

Breakdown of transactions by payment method:
- Card payments
- Bank transfers
- Digital wallets
- Local methods

### Geographic Distribution

Revenue and transaction volume by country.

## Customizing Your Dashboard

You can customize your dashboard by:
- Selecting date ranges
- Choosing specific metrics
- Filtering by payment method or country
- Creating custom views
