## Introduction

Vinr Analytics provides comprehensive insights into your payment performance and customer behavior.

## Key Metrics

### Revenue Metrics
- Total revenue
- Average transaction value
- Revenue by payment method
- Revenue by country

### Performance Metrics
- Success rate
- Decline rate
- Refund rate
- Processing time

### Customer Metrics
- New vs. returning customers
- Customer lifetime value
- Payment method preferences

## Dashboard

Access real-time analytics from your [Vinr Dashboard](https://dashboard.vinr.com/analytics).

## Time Periods

View analytics for:
- Last 24 hours
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Last 90 days
- Custom date range
