## Introduction

Vinr's billing system helps you manage invoices, subscriptions, and pricing for your business.

## Key Features

- **Automated Invoicing** - Generate and send invoices automatically
- **Subscription Management** - Manage recurring billing cycles
- **Flexible Pricing** - Set up custom pricing models
- **Tax Handling** - Automatic tax calculation and compliance

## Billing Cycle

Your billing cycle depends on your subscription plan:

- **Starter**: Monthly billing
- **Professional**: Monthly or annual billing
- **Enterprise**: Custom billing terms

## Payment Methods

You can pay your Vinr bills using:

- Credit/debit cards
- Bank transfers
- Direct debit (for annual plans)
