## Overview

View, download, and manage all your Vinr invoices from the dashboard.

## Accessing Invoices

1. Log in to your [Vinr Dashboard](https://dashboard.vinr.com)
2. Navigate to **Billing** → **Invoices**
3. View all past and upcoming invoices

## Invoice Details

Each invoice includes:

- Invoice number and date
- Billing period
- Itemized charges
- Payment method
- Tax breakdown
- Total amount

## Downloading Invoices

Click the **Download PDF** button next to any invoice to save a copy.

## Invoice Status

- **Paid** - Invoice has been paid
- **Pending** - Payment is being processed
- **Overdue** - Payment is past due
- **Void** - Invoice has been canceled
