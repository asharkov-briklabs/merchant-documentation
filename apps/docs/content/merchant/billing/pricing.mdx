## Overview

Vinr uses a transparent, pay-as-you-grow pricing model.

## Transaction Fees

### Card Payments
- **Domestic cards**: 1.4% + €0.25
- **European cards**: 1.9% + €0.25
- **International cards**: 2.9% + €0.25

### Bank Transfers
- **SEPA transfers**: €0.50 per transaction
- **Domestic transfers**: €0.30 per transaction

### Digital Wallets
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay**: 1.9% + €0.25

## Subscription Pricing

| Plan | Monthly | Annual |
|------|---------|--------|
| Starter | €29 | €290 (save 2 months) |
| Professional | €99 | €990 (save 2 months) |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom |

## Volume Discounts

High-volume merchants may qualify for discounted rates. Contact our sales team for custom pricing.
