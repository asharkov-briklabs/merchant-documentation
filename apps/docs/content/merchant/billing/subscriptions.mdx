## Overview

Vinr offers flexible subscription plans to match your business needs.

## Available Plans

### Starter
- Up to 100 transactions/month
- Basic payment methods
- Email support

### Professional
- Up to 1,000 transactions/month
- All payment methods
- Priority support
- Advanced analytics

### Enterprise
- Unlimited transactions
- Custom integrations
- Dedicated support
- SLA guarantees

## Managing Your Subscription

### Upgrade Plan

1. Navigate to **Billing** → **Subscription**
2. Click **Upgrade Plan**
3. Select your new plan
4. Confirm the change

### Downgrade Plan

1. Navigate to **Billing** → **Subscription**
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select a lower-tier plan
4. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing period
