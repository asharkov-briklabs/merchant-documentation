## Overview

Automatically sync your Vinr payment data with popular accounting platforms.

## Xero

### Setup

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Xero**
2. Click **Connect to Xero**
3. Authorize the connection
4. Map your accounts

### Features

- Automatic invoice creation
- Payment reconciliation
- Tax calculation
- Financial reporting

## QuickBooks

### Setup

1. Go to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **QuickBooks**
2. Click **Connect to QuickBooks**
3. Sign in to your QuickBooks account
4. Authorize access

### Features

- Sync customers and invoices
- Automatic transaction recording
- Real-time financial data
- Bank reconciliation

## Sage

### Setup

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Sage**
2. Enter your Sage credentials
3. Configure sync settings

## Wave

### Setup

1. Go to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Wave**
2. Connect your Wave account
3. Set up automatic syncing

## Sync Settings

Configure what data to sync:

- **Transactions** - All payment transactions
- **Refunds** - Refund records
- **Customers** - Customer information
- **Invoices** - Invoice data
