## Overview

Integrate Vinr with your CRM to track customer payment data alongside other customer interactions.

## Salesforce

### Setup

1. Install the Vinr app from Salesforce AppExchange
2. Configure API credentials
3. Map payment fields to Salesforce objects

### Features

- Customer payment history in Salesforce
- Automated customer record updates
- Payment-based lead scoring
- Revenue tracking

## HubSpot

### Setup

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **HubSpot**
2. Click **Connect to HubSpot**
3. Authorize the connection
4. Configure sync settings

### Features

- Sync payment data to contact records
- Track revenue per customer
- Create workflows based on payment events
- Revenue analytics in HubSpot

## Zoho CRM

### Setup

1. Go to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Zoho CRM**
2. Enter your Zoho credentials
3. Configure field mappings

## Pipedrive

### Setup

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Pipedrive**
2. Connect your Pipedrive account
3. Set up deal and payment sync

## Use Cases

### Customer Segmentation

Segment customers based on:
- Payment frequency
- Average transaction value
- Payment method preferences
- Refund history

### Revenue Attribution

Track which marketing campaigns drive the most revenue.

### Customer Lifecycle

Monitor customer journey from lead to paying customer.
