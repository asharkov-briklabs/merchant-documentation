## Introduction

Vinr integrates seamlessly with popular e-commerce, accounting, and CRM platforms.

## Available Integrations

### E-commerce Platforms
- WooCommerce
- Shopify
- Magento
- PrestaShop

### Accounting Software
- Xero
- QuickBooks
- Sage
- Wave

### CRM Systems
- Salesforce
- HubSpot
- Zoho CRM
- Pipedrive

## Integration Benefits

- **Automated Data Sync** - Keep your data synchronized across platforms
- **Reduced Manual Work** - Eliminate duplicate data entry
- **Better Insights** - Combine payment data with business metrics
- **Improved Accuracy** - Reduce errors from manual processes

## Getting Started

Most integrations can be set up in minutes:

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Integrations**
2. Select the platform you want to integrate
3. Follow the setup instructions
4. Authorize the connection
