## Introduction

Vinr's payment platform enables you to accept payments from customers across Bulgaria, Romania, Greece, and Poland.

## Supported Payment Methods

Vinr supports a variety of payment methods:

- **Card Payments** - Visa, Mastercard, and local card schemes
- **Bank Transfers** - Direct bank transfers (SEPA)
- **Digital Wallets** - Apple Pay, Google Pay
- **Local Methods** - Country-specific payment methods

## Key Concepts

### Payment Intent

A Payment Intent represents your intent to collect payment from a customer.

### Payment Method

A Payment Method represents a way for customers to pay.

### Confirmation

The process of confirming a payment with the customer's payment details.

## Payment Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `requires_payment_method` | Waiting for payment method |
| `requires_confirmation` | Ready to be confirmed |
| `processing` | Payment is being processed |
| `succeeded` | Payment completed successfully |
| `canceled` | Payment was canceled |
| `failed` | Payment failed |
