## Supported Payment Methods

Vinr supports various payment methods across different countries.

## Card Payments

Accept Visa, Mastercard, and local card schemes.

### Supported Cards

- Visa
- Mastercard
- Maestro
- Local card schemes (Bulgaria, Romania, Greece, Poland)

## Bank Transfers

Direct bank transfers via SEPA.

## Digital Wallets

- Apple Pay
- Google Pay

## Local Payment Methods

Country-specific payment methods for Bulgaria, Romania, Greece, and Poland.
