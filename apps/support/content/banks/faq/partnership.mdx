## Becoming a Partner

### What are the requirements to become a bank partner?

To qualify as a Vinr bank partner, you need:

- Valid banking license in Bulgaria, Romania, Greece, or Poland
- PCI DSS Level 1 compliance
- Ability to process card payments
- 24/7 operations capability
- Technical team for API integration
- Compliance and risk management systems

### How do I apply to become a partner?

1. Submit partnership application at [vinr.com/partners](https://vinr.com/partners)
2. Complete due diligence process
3. Sign partnership agreements
4. Complete technical onboarding
5. Launch integration

### How long does onboarding take?

Typical timeline:

- **Application & Due Diligence**: 2-3 weeks
- **Legal Agreements**: 1-2 weeks
- **Technical Integration**: 6-8 weeks
- **Testing & Validation**: 2-3 weeks
- **Total**: 12-16 weeks

### What documents are required?

- Banking license(s)
- Financial statements (2 years)
- PCI DSS attestation of compliance
- Articles of incorporation
- Insurance certificates
- AML/KYC policies
- Incident response procedures
- BCP/DR documentation

### Is there a minimum transaction volume?

While there's no strict minimum, we typically partner with banks that can process:

- At least 10,000 transactions per month
- Minimum €500,000 monthly volume

## Partnership Model

### How does the revenue share work?

Revenue share depends on:

- Transaction volume
- Processing capabilities
- Geographic coverage
- Value-added services

Contact our partnership team for specific terms.

### What fees does Vinr charge banks?

- Platform fee (monthly)
- Transaction fee (per transaction)
- Settlement fee (optional)
- Custom pricing available for high-volume partners

### Can I set my own merchant pricing?

You can propose merchant pricing within Vinr's guidelines. Final pricing must be approved to ensure market competitiveness.

### What support does Vinr provide?

- **Technical Support**: Integration assistance
- **Compliance Support**: Regulatory guidance
- **Account Management**: Dedicated partner manager
- **Marketing Support**: Co-marketing opportunities
- **Training**: Staff training programs

## Operating Requirements

### What are the SLA requirements?

Bank partners must maintain:

- **Uptime**: 99.9% availability
- **Response Time**: `< 2 seconds` for authorization
- **Support**: 24/7 incident response
- **Settlement**: T+2 standard

### Do I need to support all payment methods?

Minimum requirements:

- Visa and Mastercard
- 3D Secure authentication
- SEPA transfers (for EU banks)

Additional payment methods are encouraged.

### What countries must I support?

You must be licensed in at least one of:

- 🇧🇬 Bulgaria
- 🇷🇴 Romania
- 🇬🇷 Greece
- 🇵🇱 Poland

Multi-country coverage is preferred but not required.

### Can I partner if I'm not in these countries?

Currently, we only partner with banks licensed in our operating countries. However, we're expanding - contact us about future opportunities.

## Technical Requirements

### What APIs must I implement?

Required endpoints:

- Authorization API
- Capture API
- Refund API
- Void/Cancel API
- Settlement reporting
- Webhook receiver

### What security standards must I meet?

- PCI DSS Level 1 compliance
- TLS 1.2 or higher
- Strong authentication (2FA)
- Encryption at rest and in transit
- Regular security audits
- Penetration testing

### Do you provide test environments?

Yes, we provide:

- Sandbox environment
- Test merchant accounts
- Simulated transactions
- Webhook testing tools
- Complete test documentation
