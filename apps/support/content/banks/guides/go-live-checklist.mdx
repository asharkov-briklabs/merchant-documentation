## Overview

Complete this comprehensive checklist before launching your Vinr integration to production.

- Authorization success rate >95%

### Business Metrics

- Settlement accuracy 100%

- Chargeback rate `< 0.5%`
- Support ticket resolution `< 2 hours`

## Rollback Plan

If critical issues occur:

1. **Immediate Actions**
   - Switch back to previous partner
   - Notify stakeholders
   - Activate incident response

2. **Investigation**
   - Collect logs and metrics
   - Identify root cause
   - Document findings

3. **Resolution**
   - Fix issues in staging
   - Re-test thoroughly
   - Schedule new launch
