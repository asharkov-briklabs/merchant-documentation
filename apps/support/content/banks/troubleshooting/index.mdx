## Still Having Problems?

If you can't resolve your issue:

### Contact Partner Support

- **Email**: partners@vinr.com
- **Phone**: +359 2 XXX XXXX (24/7 for production issues)
- **Slack**: Partner support channel
- **Account Manager**: Your dedicated contact

### Before Contacting Support

Please have ready:

- Partner ID
- API endpoint affected
- Error messages and codes
- Request/response logs
- Steps to reproduce
- Environment (sandbox/production)

### Emergency Support

For critical production issues:

- **Email**: emergency-partners@vinr.com
- **Phone**: +359 2 XXX XXXX (24/7)
- **Response time**: Within 30 minutes
- **Escalation**: Automatic for P1 issues
