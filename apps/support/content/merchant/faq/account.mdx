## Getting Started

### How do I sign up for a Vinr account?

1. Visit [vinr.com/signup](https://vinr.com/signup)
2. Enter your business details
3. Verify your email address
4. Complete KYC verification
5. Start accepting payments

### What documents do I need to verify my account?

You'll need:

- Business registration documents
- Tax identification number
- Bank account details
- Director/owner identification
- Business license (if applicable)

### How long does verification take?

- **Standard verification**: 1-2 business days
- **Express verification**: Within 24 hours (Enterprise plans)

## Account Management

### How do I reset my password?

1. Go to [vinr.com/login](https://vinr.com/login)
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email for reset link
5. Create a new password

### Can I have multiple users on one account?

Yes! You can add team members with different permission levels:

- **Admin**: Full access
- **Developer**: API access only
- **Finance**: Billing and reporting
- **Support**: Read-only access

To add users:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Team**
2. Click **Invite User**
3. Enter email and select role
4. User receives invitation email

### How do I change my business information?

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Business Details**
2. Update your information
3. Click **Save Changes**
4. Some changes may require reverification

### Can I delete my account?

Yes, but please note:

- All pending payouts must be completed
- Subscription must be canceled
- Data will be retained for 7 years (regulatory requirement)

To delete your account, contact support@vinr.com

## Security

### How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?

1. Go to **Settings** → **Security**
2. Click **Enable 2FA**
3. Scan QR code with authenticator app
4. Enter verification code
5. Save backup codes

### What should I do if my account is compromised?

1. Immediately change your password
2. Revoke all API keys
3. Contact support@vinr.com
4. Review recent account activity
5. Enable 2FA if not already enabled

### How do I manage API keys?

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **API Keys**
2. View existing keys or create new ones
3. Use test keys for development
4. Use live keys for production
5. Rotate keys regularly for security
