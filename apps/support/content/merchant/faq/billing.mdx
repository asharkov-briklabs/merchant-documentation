## Subscription & Pricing

### What subscription plans do you offer?

| Plan | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Starter | €29/month | Up to 100 transactions |
| Professional | €99/month | Up to 1,000 transactions |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited transactions |

### Can I change my plan?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade anytime:

- **Upgrades**: Take effect immediately
- **Downgrades**: Take effect at next billing cycle

### Do you offer annual billing?

Yes! Save 2 months by paying annually:

- Starter: €290/year (vs €348)
- Professional: €990/year (vs €1,188)
- Enterprise: Custom pricing

### Is there a free trial?

Yes! All new accounts get:

- 14-day free trial
- No credit card required
- Full access to all features
- Up to 100 test transactions

## Billing & Payments

### When will I be charged?

- **Monthly**: On the same day each month
- **Annual**: On your subscription anniversary
- **Transaction fees**: Deducted from each settlement

### What payment methods do you accept for subscriptions?

- Credit/debit cards
- Bank transfers (annual plans)
- Direct debit (select countries)

### How do I update my billing information?

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. Click **Update Payment Method**
3. Enter new card details
4. Click **Save**

### Where can I download my invoices?

1. Navigate to **Billing** → **Invoices**
2. Find the invoice you need
3. Click **Download PDF**

Invoices are also emailed automatically after each billing cycle.

## Transaction Fees

### How are transaction fees calculated?

Transaction fees are percentage-based plus a fixed amount:

```
Fee = (Amount × Percentage) + Fixed Amount
```

Example: €100 payment with 1.4% + €0.25 fee
```
Fee = (€100 × 0.014) + €0.25 = €1.65
```

### Are transaction fees refunded?

No, transaction fees are not refunded when you issue a refund to a customer.

### Can I get volume discounts?

Yes! High-volume merchants may qualify for reduced rates. Contact sales@vinr.com for custom pricing.

## Payouts

### When do I receive payouts?

Standard payout schedule:

- **Daily**: For Enterprise plans
- **Weekly**: For Professional plans
- **Monthly**: For Starter plans

### Can I change my payout schedule?

Yes, upgrade to a higher plan for more frequent payouts, or contact support to customize your schedule.

### Where are payouts sent?

Payouts are sent to the bank account you provided during signup. To update:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Payout Account**
2. Verify your identity
3. Add new bank account details

### Why is my payout delayed?

Common reasons:

- Bank holiday
- Verification pending
- High-risk transaction review
- Chargeback reserve

Check your dashboard for specific details or contact support.

## Cancellation & Refunds

### How do I cancel my subscription?

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Confirm cancellation
4. Subscription remains active until end of billing period

### Can I get a refund?

- **Subscription fees**: Refundable within 30 days
- **Transaction fees**: Non-refundable
- **Annual plans**: Pro-rated refund available

### What happens to my data after cancellation?

- Account data retained for 90 days
- Transaction data retained for 7 years (regulatory requirement)
- You can export your data anytime before deletion
