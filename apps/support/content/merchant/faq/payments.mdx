## Payment Processing

### What payment methods do you support?

Vinr supports:

- **Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, local card schemes
- **Bank Transfers**: SEPA, domestic transfers
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay
- **Local Methods**: Country-specific payment methods

### Which countries do you support?

Currently, we support merchants in:

- 🇧🇬 Bulgaria
- 🇷🇴 Romania
- 🇬🇷 Greece
- 🇵🇱 Poland

### What currencies can I accept?

- EUR (Euro)
- BGN (Bulgarian Lev)
- RON (Romanian Leu)
- PLN (Polish Złoty)

### How long do payments take to process?

- **Card payments**: Instant authorization, settlement in 1-2 business days
- **Bank transfers**: 1-3 business days
- **Digital wallets**: Instant

### What are your transaction fees?

| Payment Method | Fee |
|----------------|-----|
| Domestic cards | 1.4% + €0.25 |
| European cards | 1.9% + €0.25 |
| International cards | 2.9% + €0.25 |
| SEPA transfers | €0.50 |
| Digital wallets | 1.9% + €0.25 |

Volume discounts available for high-volume merchants.

## Payment Issues

### Why was a payment declined?

Common reasons:

- Insufficient funds
- Incorrect card details
- Expired card
- Card reported lost or stolen
- Suspected fraud
- Daily limit exceeded

Check the decline reason in your dashboard for specific details.

### Can customers pay in installments?

Yes! Vinr supports installment payments in select countries. Contact sales for details.

### Do you support recurring payments?

Yes, Vinr supports:

- Subscription billing
- Recurring charges
- Payment plans
- Automatic renewals

## Refunds

### How do I issue a refund?

1. Go to **Payments** in your dashboard
2. Find the payment
3. Click **Refund**
4. Enter refund amount (full or partial)
5. Confirm refund

### How long do refunds take?

- **Card refunds**: 5-10 business days
- **Bank transfer refunds**: 3-5 business days

### Can I refund part of a payment?

Yes, partial refunds are supported. You can refund any amount up to the original payment amount.

### Is there a fee for refunds?

The processing fee is not refunded, but there's no additional fee to process a refund.

## 3D Secure

### What is 3D Secure?

3D Secure is an additional layer of security for online card payments that requires customer authentication.

### Is 3D Secure required?

3D Secure is automatically applied when required by:

- The card issuer
- Transaction risk level
- Regulatory requirements (SCA in Europe)

### Why do some payments skip 3D Secure?

Low-risk payments may qualify for exemptions under PSD2 rules.
